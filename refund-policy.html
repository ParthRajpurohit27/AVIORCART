We want you to love what you order. If something isn't right, here's exactly how cancellations, returns and refunds work at AVIORCART.

1.Order Cancellation

  • You may request cancellation of an order as long as it has not yet been shipped. Contact us as soon as possible after placing your order to request a cancellation.
  • Once an order has been shipped, it can no longer be cancelled — you may instead return it after delivery, subject to the return terms below.
  • Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method within the timeline described in the "Refunds" section.

2.Returns

We offer 10 days easy returns from the date of delivery on eligible products.

To be eligible for a return, an item must be:

  • Unused, unworn and in its original condition
  • In the original packaging, with all tags and accessories intact
  • Requested within 10 days of the delivery date

To start a return, contact us with your order ID and the reason for return. We'll guide you through the pickup or drop-off process.

3.Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following categories are not eligible for return unless the item arrives damaged or defective:

  • Innerwear, cosmetics and personal-care/beauty products that have been opened or used
  • Earrings and other pierced jewellery
  • Items marked "Non-Returnable" on the product page

4.Refunds

  • Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll notify you of the approval status of your refund.
  • Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment method via PayU, typically within 5–7 business days, though your bank may take a few additional days to reflect it.
  • For cancelled orders (before shipping), refunds are processed within 3–5 business days.

5.Damaged, Defective or Wrong Item

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and packaging. We'll arrange a free replacement or a full refund, at your preference, at no extra cost to you.
