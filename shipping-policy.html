🚚 Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains how we ship your order, how long it takes, and what to do if something looks off.

1.Delivery Coverage

We currently ship to serviceable pincodes across India through our courier partners. Availability at your exact pincode is confirmed automatically at checkout.

2.Shipping Charges

We currently offer free delivery on all orders, for a limited time — no minimum order value required.

Any future changes to shipping charges will be clearly displayed at checkout before you complete your payment.

3.Processing & Delivery Timelines

  • Order processing: orders are typically processed and handed to our courier partner within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Delivery: most orders are delivered within 4–9 business days, depending on your location.
  • Timelines may vary for remote areas, during sale periods, or due to circumstances outside our control (weather, courier delays, regional restrictions, etc.).

4.Order Tracking

Since AVIORCART doesn't require an account to shop, we don't yet offer an automated tracking page. Once your order ships, contact us with your order ID (from your order confirmation) and we'll share the live courier tracking details with you directly.

5.Delays & Failed Delivery

  • If a delivery attempt fails because you were unavailable or the address was incomplete, our courier partner will typically make up to 2–3 more attempts before returning the shipment to us.
  • Please ensure your phone number and address are accurate and complete at checkout — this is the most common cause of delivery delays.
  • If your order hasn't arrived within the estimated window, contact us and we'll look into it right away.
