AVIORCART ("we", "us", "our") operates the website and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using AVIORCART, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1.Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you place an order or contact us, including:

  • Full name, phone number and email address
  • Delivery address (address line, city, state, pincode)
  • Order details — items purchased, quantities and amount paid
  • Any message you send us through our contact form or WhatsApp

We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or net-banking details. Payments are processed entirely by our payment partner, PayU — see the "Payments" section below.

2.How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your order to the address you provide
  • Send order confirmations and updates related to your purchase
  • Respond to customer service requests and support queries
  • Improve our website, products and overall shopping experience
  • Comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting records

3.Payments

All payments on AVIORCART are processed by PayU, a licensed and PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. When you check out, you are redirected to PayU's secure payment page — we never see or store your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials. PayU shares back with us only the transaction status, transaction ID and amount, so we can confirm and fulfil your order.

Your delivery address and order summary are transmitted securely to us at the moment your payment is verified, so your order can be processed immediately — without you needing to take any further action after paying.

4.Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your information with:

  • PayU — to process your payment securely
  • Delivery/courier partners — to ship your order to you
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law

5.Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse, including transmitting data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and restricting access to order information to authorised personnel only.

6.Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we are not legally required to retain it

To exercise any of these rights, reach out using the contact details below.

7.Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember items in your shopping cart and wishlist between visits. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us until you place an order. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

8.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.
